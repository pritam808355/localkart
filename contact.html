Connecting Communities with Local Commerce

LocalKart is dedicated to bridging the digital divide for small shops, artisan makers, and local businesses — enabling transparent discovery, genuine savings, and direct community relationships.

Our Purpose & Story

The Real-World Problem We Solve

In an era where massive e-commerce conglomerates dominate online sales with aggressive warehousing and 20% to 35% commission fees, neighborhood stores face serious visibility hurdles. Despite having exceptional organic produce, pure cold-pressed staples, handmade textiles, and generational culinary skills, local shopkeepers remain invisible to nearby residents searching online.

LocalKart changes this. We offer a zero-commission hyperlocal discovery marketplace where customers can find nearby brick-and-mortar stores, inspect available products with verified local pricing, and connect directly with shopkeepers via phone or WhatsApp.
